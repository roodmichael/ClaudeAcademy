# Lab 04 Procedure: Origin Comparison

**Estimated time:** Two roast sessions (~15-20 min each) + 24-48h rest + 45 min comparative cupping
**Beans needed:** 100-120g of your natural-process bean + 100-120g of your washed bean (green weight each)

---

## Part A: Two Roasts, Same Level

Your goal is to roast both beans as identically as possible in terms of development level. This means the same heat profile, same charge weight, and the same stop point relative to first crack.

### Important Note About Different Beans

Natural and washed coffees can behave slightly differently in the roaster. The Durato Bombe G1 (natural) may have slightly different first crack timing and characteristics than the Mexico washed. This is expected and worth noting — but your stop point should be the same number of seconds past first crack end for both.

If one bean's first crack ends significantly earlier or later than the other, that is data. Log it.

### Roast Sequence

**Before either roast:**
- Decide on your target stop point (e.g., 45 seconds after first crack ends — whatever worked well for you in Lab 03)
- Set your heat and airflow identically for both
- Weigh 100g of each bean separately

**Roast 1: Natural-Process Bean**
Follow your standard procedure. Document all timeline points. Drop at your target development time. Label: "Lab 04 — Roast 1 — [Bean Name] Natural — [date]"

Allow the machine to return to charge temperature before starting Roast 2 (or use the same batch session if your machine allows back-to-back roasts).

**Roast 2: Washed Bean**
Same procedure, same settings. Document all timeline points. Note any differences in how this bean behaves compared to the natural-process bean. Drop at the same development time past first crack end. Label: "Lab 04 — Roast 2 — [Bean Name] Washed — [date]"

### After Both Roasts

- Weigh and calculate weight loss for each. Compare.
- While both are fresh, smell them side by side. Note any differences in color or aroma at this stage.
- Rest both for 24-48 hours in separate labeled containers.

## Part C: Side-by-Side Cupping

Cup both simultaneously, following the same comparative protocol from Lab 03.

### Setup

- Grind 8g of each to the same coarse grind
- Two labeled cups: N (Natural) and W (Washed)
- Brew simultaneously
- 4 minutes, break both crusts, skim, taste

### What to Evaluate

Complete the full post-roast evaluation for each. Then in your session notes, answer these comparative questions:

1. **The clearest difference:** What is the single biggest flavor difference between the two cups? Where does it sit on the flavor wheel?

2. **Acidity character:** Is the acidity in the Ethiopia different in type from the Mexico, or just in amount? (Citric/fruity vs. sharp vs. crisp vs. absent)

3. **Sweetness type:** Is the sweetness in the Ethiopia fruity? Is the sweetness in the Mexico more like caramel or chocolate? Or is one not sweet at all?

4. **Body comparison:** Which cup feels heavier? Natural-processed coffees often have more body due to residual sugars — do you taste that here?

5. **The processing difference:** Can you actually taste what it means for coffee to be dried on vs. dried off the fruit? Describe it in your own words.

6. **If you had not known the origins:** Would you have recognized these as different beans from different countries? Why or why not?
