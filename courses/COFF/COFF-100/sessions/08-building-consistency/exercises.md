# Session 08 Exercises

Complete these before taking the session quiz and before beginning the Final Project.

---

## Exercise 1: Roast Log Retrospective
**Type:** Analysis

Review all of your roasting and tasting notes from Sessions 06 and 07. Then answer the following:

1. What was the most significant thing you learned from your first roast that you couldn't have predicted from the reading alone?
2. Looking at your log, what specific number changed between your initial plan and your actual roast? (e.g., development time, end temperature, total roast time)
3. Identify one roast decision that you made deliberately and one that you made reactively (in response to something unexpected). What was each?
4. If you have done more than one roast since Session 06, compare two batches. What was the most important difference between them in tasting notes?

Write 1–2 sentences per question.

---

## Exercise 2: The Single-Variable Experiment Plan
**Type:** Planning

Design your next two roasts as a controlled single-variable experiment.

**Starting point:** Your best roast so far.

**Variable to test:** Choose ONE of the following:
- Development time (end roast 45–60 seconds earlier in Roast B vs. Roast A)
- Green coffee origin (same process, same roast profile, different origin)
- Batch size (same coffee, same process, 15% more coffee by weight in Roast B)

**Write your experiment plan:**
1. What is the variable you're testing?
2. What is the specific change between Roast A and Roast B?
3. What do you predict will be different in the cup? (Make an actual prediction before you roast.)
4. How will you evaluate the result? What specifically will you compare?

You don't need to execute this plan before the quiz — but you should execute it within the next two weeks as part of your ongoing practice.

---

## Exercise 3: Your Personal Roasting Standard
**Type:** Reflection / Planning

Write a short "roasting standard" for yourself — a brief document (10–15 sentences) that describes your current best practice. This is not about what you hope to achieve eventually. It's about what you now know to be true from this course and your own experience.

Include:
1. Your preferred roast level and why
2. What green coffee characteristics matter most to you right now (origin, processing, freshness)
3. Your pre-roast setup routine (what you always do before starting)
4. How you determine when to stop the roast
5. Your cooling and storage practice
6. Your tasting protocol (how you evaluate each batch)
7. The one thing you're still working to improve

This document becomes the first page of your ongoing roasting journal. Return to it after Batch 10, Batch 20, and Batch 50. Notice how it changes.
