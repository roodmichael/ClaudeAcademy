# Session 01 Exercises

Complete these before taking the session quiz.

---

## Exercise 1: The Freshness Test
**Type:** Hands-on / Sensory observation

Find two coffees in your home or nearby: one bag of supermarket or grocery-store ground coffee (check the roast date on the bag — many have one), and one bag from a local specialty roaster or online roaster if possible. If you only have one type, that's fine — use it for the observation.

Open each bag and smell them separately. Take at least 30 seconds with each.

**What to observe / record:**

- What does each coffee smell like? Describe the aroma in your own words — don't worry about using "correct" vocabulary yet.
- If you can find the roast date: how many days ago was each coffee roasted?
- If one smells more vibrant or interesting, which one? What's different about it?
- Write 2–3 sentences about what you noticed. There's no wrong answer here — you're building your observational vocabulary.

---

## Exercise 2: The Three Waves in Your Life
**Type:** Thought experiment / Reflection

Think about your own relationship with coffee up until now.

**What to observe / record:**

Answer these questions in writing (a few sentences each is enough):

1. What kind of coffee did you grow up with, or start drinking? Was it instant, drip from a can, café lattes, or something else?
2. Have you ever had coffee that tasted dramatically different from what you expected — better or worse? What do you remember about it?
3. What made you curious about home roasting? What do you hope to be able to do differently after this course?

There's nothing to get right or wrong here. The goal is to start making your current coffee experience visible — so you can track how it changes.

---

## Exercise 3: The Roast Date Hunt
**Type:** Analysis

Check the last three bags of coffee you've purchased or currently have at home (or check a local grocery store). Look for:
- A "roast date" (often printed near the bottom or back of the bag)
- A "best by" date (less useful, but common)
- No date at all

**What to observe / record:**

- How many bags had a roast date? How many had only a "best by" date? How many had neither?
- For those that had a roast date: how many days had passed between roasting and when you opened or purchased the bag?
- What does this tell you about the freshness of most commercially available coffee?

Write a short conclusion (2–4 sentences).
