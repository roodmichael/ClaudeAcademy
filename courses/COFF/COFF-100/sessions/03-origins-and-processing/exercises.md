# Session 03 Exercises

Complete these before taking the session quiz.

---

## Exercise 1: Region-Flavor Matching
**Type:** Analysis / Thought experiment

Without looking back at the lesson, try to match each coffee description to its most likely region of origin. Write your answers and then check them against the session.

**Coffee descriptions:**
1. "Earthy, full-bodied, low acidity, with herbal and almost mushroomy notes."
2. "Intensely floral — jasmine and bergamot on the nose, bright berry fruit in the cup."
3. "Balanced and approachable — mild caramel sweetness, soft nutty character, pleasant but not aggressive."
4. "Bold, juicy acidity with wine-like berry notes. Almost shockingly bright."
5. "Chocolatey, medium-to-full body, mild spice, approachable across a wide range of roast levels."

**Regions to choose from:** Ethiopia, Colombia, Brazil, Kenya, Guatemala, Sumatra (Indonesia)

**After matching:** Which of these descriptions sounds most appealing to you right now? Which sounds least familiar? Write 2–3 sentences about what that tells you about your current flavor preferences.

## Exercise 2: Processing Method Prediction
**Type:** Thought experiment

You are going to roast two coffees from the same country (Ethiopia) — one washed, one natural — using the exact same roasting profile (same temperature, same time, same equipment).

**Answer these questions before roasting (you'll revisit them in Sessions 06 and 07):**

1. Based on what you learned about washed vs. natural processing, predict: which coffee do you expect to taste brighter and more acidic? Which do you expect to taste sweeter and more fruit-forward?
2. Do you think the processing method will affect how the beans look or sound during roasting? Why or why not?
3. Write down what specific flavor differences you're going to pay attention to when you taste these coffees in Session 07.

## Exercise 3: Sourcing Your Green Coffee
**Type:** Hands-on / Planning

Visit at least one green coffee supplier (Sweet Maria's, Coffee Bean Corral, or Burman Coffee Traders). Browse their current selection and identify 2–3 coffees you'd like to try.

For each coffee, note:
1. Country and region
2. Processing method
3. Altitude
4. Flavor descriptors offered by the supplier
5. Price per pound

Then answer: If you could only choose one for your first roast, which would it be and why? Write 3–5 sentences explaining your reasoning, using vocabulary from this session (origin, processing method, flavor profile).

If you have already purchased green coffee, complete this exercise using your existing beans and one additional coffee from a supplier listing.
