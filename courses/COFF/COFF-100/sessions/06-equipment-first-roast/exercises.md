# Session 06 Exercises

These exercises are the core of this session. Complete Exercise 1 before your roast. Complete Exercises 2 and 3 during and immediately after.

---

## Exercise 1: Workspace Setup and Pre-Roast Checklist
**Type:** Hands-on / Planning

Before you roast, complete this checklist. Check off each item physically.

**Equipment ready:**
- [ ] Roasting device prepped (popper, skillet, or dedicated roaster)
- [ ] Kitchen scale on counter
- [ ] Timer ready (phone stopwatch is fine)
- [ ] Colander or cooling tray in place
- [ ] Large bowl for chaff collection
- [ ] Ventilation open (window and/or fan)
- [ ] Notebook or roasting log open

**Green coffee ready:**
- [ ] Beans weighed and weight recorded: _______ grams
- [ ] Origin noted in log: _______
- [ ] Processing method noted: _______
- [ ] Any defects picked out? Y / N — how many removed: _______

**Pre-roast plan written:**
- [ ] Target roast level: _______
- [ ] Planned end point: _______ (e.g., "90 seconds after first crack ends")

**What to record:** Complete this checklist in writing and keep it as the header entry in your roasting log.

## Exercise 3: Post-Roast Documentation
**Type:** Hands-on

Complete this immediately after your roast, before storing the beans.

1. **Roasted bean weight:** _______ grams
2. **Weight loss:** _______ grams (green weight − roasted weight)
3. **Weight loss percentage:** _______ % (weight loss ÷ green weight × 100)
4. **Bean color assessment** (circle one): Light / Medium-Light / Medium / Medium-Dark / Dark
5. **Aroma of cooled beans** (describe in 2–3 words): _______
6. **Storage label written?** Y / N — includes: origin, roast date, roast level
7. **Estimated drink date** (roast date + 48 hours minimum): _______

**Reflection questions** (write 3–5 sentences total):
- What was the most surprising thing about doing the roast vs. reading about it?
- Did first crack sound the way you expected?
- What one thing would you do differently on your next roast?
