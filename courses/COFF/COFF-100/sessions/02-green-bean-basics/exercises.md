# Session 02 Exercises

Complete these before taking the session quiz.

---

## Exercise 1: Green Bean Examination
**Type:** Hands-on / Sensory observation

If you have received your green coffee beans, take out a small handful (about 20–30 beans).

**What to observe / record:**

1. **Color:** What color are they? Consistent, or do some look different?
2. **Smell:** Describe the smell. What does it remind you of? (Common descriptions: grass, hay, fresh vegetables, earthy.)
3. **Size and uniformity:** Are the beans roughly the same size? Pick out any that look significantly different.
4. **Defects check:** Look for any black beans, shells (beans that are hollow or look like a fragment), or obviously broken beans. How many did you find out of your sample?
5. **Weight:** If you have a scale, weigh 10 beans. Note the weight. You'll compare this later to roasted beans of the same origin.

If your green beans haven't arrived yet, complete Exercise 2 and 3 now and return to Exercise 1 when they arrive.

## Exercise 2: Species Comparison
**Type:** Analysis / Thought experiment

Read the following two coffee descriptions and answer the questions below:

**Coffee A:** Grown at 1,800 meters in Ethiopia's Yirgacheffe region. Described as having notes of jasmine, lemon, and blueberry.

**Coffee B:** Grown in Vietnam's lowland regions. Used primarily in a large-batch espresso blend for its strong body and crema production.

**Questions:**
1. Which coffee is most likely arabica? Which is most likely robusta? What clues in the descriptions tell you this?
2. If you were choosing a green coffee for your first home roasting experiment, which type would you choose and why?
3. Based on what you learned about arabica's history, why do you think it tends to be grown at higher altitudes?

Write 3–5 sentences total.

## Exercise 3: Reading a Green Coffee Listing
**Type:** Analysis

Go to Sweet Maria's (sweetmarias.com), Coffee Bean Corral (coffeebeancorral.com), or any specialty green coffee supplier website. Find any single-origin green coffee listing.

**What to observe / record:**

Write down the following information from the listing (as much as is available):
1. Country and region of origin
2. Processing method (washed, natural, honey, or other)
3. Altitude
4. Variety (if listed — e.g., Bourbon, Typica, Geisha)
5. Flavor descriptors listed by the seller
6. Suggested roast level (if provided)

Then write 2–3 sentences: what does this information tell you about what to expect from this coffee? What are you still unsure about?

(Note: This exercise prepares you for Session 03, which explains exactly what all these terms mean.)
