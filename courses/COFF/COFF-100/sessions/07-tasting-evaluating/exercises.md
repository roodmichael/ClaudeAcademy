# Session 07 Exercises

These exercises require your roasted coffee from Session 06. Wait at least 48 hours after roasting before completing Exercise 1.

---

## Exercise 1: Your First Cupping Session
**Type:** Hands-on / Sensory observation

Set up a cupping session with your Session 06 roasted coffee. If you have a second coffee available (a commercial specialty coffee, or a different origin), set up two cups for comparison.

**Setup:**
- Grind approximately 8g of each coffee to a coarse grind
- Heat water to approximately 93°C (200°F) — just off boil
- Have your tasting notes page ready

**Conduct the cupping using the Session 07 protocol. Record the following for each coffee:**

**Dry aroma** (before water): _______

**Wet aroma** (when you break the crust): _______

**Acidity** (1 = flat, 5 = very bright): _______ Description: _______

**Sweetness** (1 = absent, 5 = very sweet): _______ Description: _______

**Body** (1 = thin/watery, 5 = thick/heavy): _______ Description: _______

**Aftertaste**: Positive (lingering pleasant flavor) / Negative (bitter, harsh, empty) / Neutral

**Balance**: Everything working together (Y/N)? What's dominant if not balanced?

**Overall flavor notes** (use the flavor wheel vocabulary if you can, your own words if not): _______

**Overall impression** (circle): Under-developed / Well-executed / Over-developed

## Exercise 2: The Feedback Connection
**Type:** Analysis

Pull out your Session 06 roasting log. Now answer these questions by cross-referencing your tasting notes:

1. What was the most noticeable flavor attribute in your first roasted batch — positive or negative?
2. Based on the flavor defect map from Session 07, what does this suggest about your roast?
3. Looking at your roast log: what was your total roast time? Your development time (time from first crack start to end of roast)?
4. If you were to roast this same coffee again tomorrow, what ONE specific change would you make and why?

Write 4–6 sentences connecting the taste result to the roast process. Be specific.

---

## Exercise 3: Flavor Wheel Exploration
**Type:** Sensory observation / Analysis

Download or find the SCA Coffee Taster's Flavor Wheel online (sca.coffee).

Taste your cupped coffee again after it has cooled to near room temperature.

Work through the wheel, starting at the center:
1. Which broad flavor family (center ring) is most present?
2. Navigate to the middle ring. Which subcategory fits best?
3. If you can, navigate to the outer ring. What specific descriptor, if any, matches?
4. Repeat for the second-most prominent flavor.

Write your result as two flavor descriptors. Example: "Predominantly nutty — hazelnut, with a secondary caramel sweetness."

Then: how does this compare to the origin flavor notes your green coffee supplier described? Are there any similarities?
