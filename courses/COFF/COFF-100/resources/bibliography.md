# Bibliography — COFF-100: Home Coffee Roasting Foundations

Consolidated from all five research documents. Sources are grouped by tier. All sources accessed 2026-04-05.

---

## Tier 1 — Official / Authoritative Organizations

**National Coffee Association / AboutCoffee.org**
"History of Coffee." https://www.aboutcoffee.org/origins/history-of-coffee/
*Covers: Coffee's origins in Ethiopia and Yemen, spread to the Arabian Peninsula and Europe, American coffee history, the three waves of coffee culture. Primary source for Session 01.*

**Specialty Coffee Association (SCA)**
"Coffee Taster's Flavor Wheel." https://sca.coffee/research/coffee-tasters-flavor-wheel
*Covers: The SCA/WCR Coffee Taster's Flavor Wheel (2016 revision), the World Coffee Research Sensory Lexicon, and the standard vocabulary for evaluating coffee flavor. Primary source for Sessions 07 and research documents 02, 03, and 05.*

**Specialty Coffee Association (SCA)**
"SCA Coffee Standards." https://sca.coffee/research/coffee-standards
*Covers: Current published and in-development SCA standards, including SCA-310 (Home Coffee Brewers) and the in-development SCA-130 (Roast Level Designations). Noted in research that roast level designation standard is not yet finalized.*

---

## Tier 3 — Recognized Industry Institutions and Publications

**Perfect Daily Grind**
"A Beginner's Guide to Roasting Great Coffee at Home." https://perfectdailygrind.com/2020/05/a-beginners-guide-to-roasting-great-coffee-at-home/
*Covers: Roasting stages, equipment options, roast levels, cooling, degassing, beginner tips, common mistakes. Used in multiple research documents and sessions.*

**Perfect Daily Grind**
"A Home Roaster's Guide to Buying the Right Green Coffee." https://perfectdailygrind.com/2018/08/a-home-roasters-guide-to-buying-the-right-green-coffee/
*Covers: How to evaluate and select green coffee — origin, altitude, variety, processing method, flavor relationships, and practical purchasing advice. Primary source for Sessions 03 and research-02.*

**Perfect Daily Grind**
"Washed, Natural, Honey: Coffee Processing 101." https://perfectdailygrind.com/2016/07/washed-natural-honey-coffee-processing-101/
*Covers: The three main processing methods (washed, natural, honey), their mechanics, and their flavor effects. Primary source for Session 03 and research-02.*

**Perfect Daily Grind**
"How to Roast Natural and Honey Processed Coffee." https://perfectdailygrind.com/2017/10/how-to-roast-natural-honey-processed-coffee/
*Covers: How processing method affects roasting behavior — particularly natural and honey coffees and their response to heat.*

**Sucafina**
"Chemical Reactions in Roasting." https://sucafina.com/na/news/chemical-reactions-in-roasting
*Covers: The Maillard reaction, caramelization, first crack chemistry, second crack/pyrolysis, and the role of temperature management. Primary chemistry source for Session 04 and research-03.*

**Torque Coffees**
"The Three Stages of Coffee Roasting: The Science and Craft Behind Exceptional Flavor." https://torque.coffee/blogs/brew-guides/the-three-stages-of-coffee-roasting-the-science-amp-craft-behind-exceptional-flavor-nbsp
*Covers: Detailed breakdown of the drying, Maillard, and development phases with temperature ranges, percentage of roast time, flavor diagnostics, and first/second crack explanation. Primary source for Sessions 04, 05, and research-03.*

"Roasting Coffee at Home: A Beginner's Guide." https://homesteadingfamily.com/roasting-coffee-at-home/

"The History of Coffee Roasting." https://latitudecoffee.com/blogs/explore/the-history-of-coffee-roasting-from-open-flames-to-artisan-techniques

**Rock Creek Coffee Roasters**
"Introduction to Coffee Beans: Origins, Flavour Profiles and Roasting Tips." https://rockcreekcoffee.com/blogs/blog/introduction-to-coffee-beans-their-flavour-profiles
*Covers: Overview of coffee bean varieties, origin flavor profiles, and introductory roasting guidance. Used in research-02.*

**Roastar**
"The Best Equipment for Home Roasters." https://www.roastar.com/blog/the-best-equipment-for-home-roasters
*Covers: Home roasting equipment types (popcorn popper, air roaster, drum roaster, household items), essential supporting equipment (scale, thermometer), beginner advice. Primary source for Sessions 06 and research-04.*

**Shinza Coffee Roasters**
"Specialty Coffee History in the United States." https://shinzacoffeeroasters.com/blogs/coffee-information/specialty-coffee-history-in-the-united-states

**Sweet Maria's Coffee Library**
"Air Popcorn Popper Method for Home Coffee Roasting." https://library.sweetmarias.com/air-popcorn-popper-method-for-home-coffee-roasting/
*Covers: Popcorn popper selection criteria (side-venting vs. bottom-screen), the roasting process using a popper, tips and common problems. Primary source for Sessions 06 and research-04.*

**Wikipedia**
"Coffee bean." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffee_bean
*Covers: Arabica and robusta species comparison, world production percentages, chemical composition of green coffee. Used in research-02.*

**Wikipedia**
"Coffea canephora." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coffea_canephora
*Covers: Robusta species origin, growing conditions, flavor comparison to arabica. Referenced in research-02.*

## Notes on Source Quality

The research for COFF-100 achieved Tier 1 coverage through the NCA (coffee history) and the SCA (flavor wheel and standards). Chemistry coverage relies primarily on Tier 3 industry publications (Sucafina, Torque Coffees, Perfect Daily Grind) that accurately reflect practitioner and industry consensus. Equipment coverage is primarily Tier 5 (practitioner sites), as no independent Tier 1-3 comparative equipment testing was found. No blocked domains were used. See research/index.md for the full coverage gap assessment.
