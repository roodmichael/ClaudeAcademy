## Key Themes

- **Freshness is the primary value of home roasting** — roasting your own beans guarantees access to coffee at peak flavor, within days of roasting rather than months. This is the central "why" for beginners.
- **The three roasting stages are the foundation of all skill development** — understanding drying, browning, and development phases allows the student to diagnose and fix problems systematically.
- **First crack is the essential landmark** — it is audible, exothermic, and definitive. All roast level decisions are made relative to it.
- **Green bean selection matters as much as roasting technique** — origin, altitude, and processing method determine the flavor ceiling; roasting reveals or diminishes what the bean contains.
- **The feedback loop of roasting is: roast → taste → adjust → repeat** — the roast log and tasting notes are inseparable tools for improvement.

1. Why home roast? History and the case for freshness
2. Understanding the coffee cherry: green bean basics
3. Origins and processing methods: what your beans bring to the roast
4. The roasting process: stages and chemistry
5. Reading the roast: first crack, second crack, and roast levels
6. Equipment and your first roast
7. Tasting and evaluating your roasts
8. Building consistency: logging, adjusting, and improving

Eight sessions is appropriate for a standard-depth Foundations course. Fewer than 8 sessions would compress either the green bean knowledge or the tasting feedback loop — both are essential foundations.
