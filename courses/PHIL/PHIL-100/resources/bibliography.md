## Coverage Notes

All primary texts used in the course are in the public domain and freely available at MIT Classics (classics.mit.edu) or Perseus Digital Library (perseus.tufts.edu). Students are not required to purchase any materials.

The Diels-Kranz numbering system (DK) is used throughout for Pre-Socratic fragments — DK22 = Heraclitus, DK28 = Parmenides, DK12 = Anaximander. The standard English collection of Pre-Socratic fragments is Kirk, Raven, and Schofield (see Tier 4 above).
