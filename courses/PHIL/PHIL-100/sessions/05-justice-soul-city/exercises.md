# Session 05 Exercises

Complete these before taking the session quiz.

## Exercise 1: The Ring of Gyges
**Type:** Thought experiment

In Republic Book II, Glaucon presents the Ring of Gyges: a ring that makes the wearer invisible. He challenges Socrates to prove that a just person would still act justly even if invisible — even when no one could ever know.

**What to observe / record:**
1. If you had the Ring of Gyges, would you act differently? Be honest.
2. If you would act the same, why? What is motivating you if not reputation or consequences?
3. If you would act differently, does Plato's argument change your view — or do you think the question of justice is fundamentally about external consequences?

Write 2–3 paragraphs. This is a genuine reflection, not a test. There is no correct answer.

## Exercise 2: Tripartite Soul in Action
**Type:** Analysis

Think of a recent decision you made where different parts of you pulled in different directions — a situation involving conflicting desires, emotions, and reasoning.

Map it onto Plato's tripartite soul:
- What was your *appetite* saying? (What did you want, immediately and physically?)
- What was your *spirit* saying? (What did your sense of pride, honor, or righteous indignation say?)
- What was your *reason* saying? (What did your long-term judgment advise?)

**What to observe / record:**
- Which part "won"? Are you satisfied with that outcome?
- Does the tripartite model feel like an accurate description of your inner life, or does it miss something important?

Write 1–2 paragraphs.

## Exercise 3: Thrasymachus and Justice
**Type:** Analysis / Close Reading

Find Thrasymachus' main argument in Republic Book I (it appears after his angry entrance, around 338c–344c). Read it carefully.

**What to observe / record:**
1. State Thrasymachus' argument in your own words. Make it as strong as possible — don't caricature it.
2. What is Socrates' main response?
3. Do you find Socrates' response convincing? Is Thrasymachus' position fully refuted, or does something of it survive?

Write 3–5 sentences for each question.
