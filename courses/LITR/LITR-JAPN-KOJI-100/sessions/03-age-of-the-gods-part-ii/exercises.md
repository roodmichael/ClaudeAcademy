# Session 03 Exercises

Complete these before taking the session quiz.

## Exercise 1: Read the Rampage and Track the Escalation
**Type:** Reading + Analysis

Open your Chamberlain to the Ama-no-Iwato section and read Susanoo's rampage carefully.
The lesson summarizes it, but the text has details worth examining directly.

**What to record:**

1. The lesson notes that Amaterasu initially makes excuses for Susanoo's behavior with
   the rice paddies. Find this moment in the text. What excuse does she offer? What does
   it tell you about her character that she tries to find a benign interpretation?

2. The destruction escalates across three targets: paddies, sacred hall, weaving hall.
   Each is more sacred than the last. Why do you think the text structures the rampage
   as an escalation rather than a single act?

3. Chamberlain uses Latin for the explicit content in the weaving hall scene. Note where
   the Latin appears. Even without reading it, what does the presence of untranslated
   Latin tell you about the nature of the original passage?

## Exercise 2: The Strategy of Laughter
**Type:** Thought experiment + Written reflection

The gods' strategy for getting Amaterasu out of the cave is not force — it is laughter
and curiosity. They create a spectacle so joyous and incomprehensible that Amaterasu must
look.

**Reflect and write a short paragraph (5–8 sentences):**

Why is laughter an effective tool for drawing someone out of withdrawal or hiding? What
does curiosity do that force cannot? You can think about this psychologically, socially,
or politically. You might also consider: why does the mirror specifically — seeing her
own reflection — freeze Amaterasu? What does being confronted with an image of yourself do?

## Exercise 3: The Three Imperial Regalia
**Type:** Analysis

The Ama-no-Iwato episode produces two of the three imperial regalia (the mirror and the
magatama beads). The third (the sword Kusanagi) comes from Susanoo's slaying of Yamata no
Orochi, which you should have read for this session.

**Questions to answer:**

1. Map each of the three regalia to its mythological origin: which episode did it come
   from, and who produced or found it?

2. Why do you think physical objects — a mirror, jewels, a sword — become tokens of
   imperial legitimacy? What does it mean politically and ritually for a ruler to possess
   objects with divine origins?

3. In your Chamberlain, find the moment Susanoo presents the sword to Amaterasu after
   slaying Yamata no Orochi. What words does he use, and what does the gesture of
   presenting the sword upward (to heaven) suggest about the relationship between
   Susanoo and Amaterasu at that moment?
