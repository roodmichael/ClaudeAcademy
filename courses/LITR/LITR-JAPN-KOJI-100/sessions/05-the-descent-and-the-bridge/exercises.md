## Exercise 1: Read the Three-Regalia Transfer
**Type:** Reading + Analysis

Find in your Chamberlain the scene where Amaterasu gives Ninigi the three imperial regalia
before his descent.

**What to record:**

1. What specific instructions does Amaterasu give Ninigi about how to use or regard each
   object? Read carefully — the instructions are brief but specific, especially regarding
   the mirror.

2. Compare this transfer of objects to the beginning of the Kojiki, where the Heavenly
   Deities give Izanagi and Izanami the jeweled spear before their creative work. What
   structural parallel do you see? What does it suggest about how the Kojiki uses
   the gift of a divine object?

3. Note which of the regalia was made by the heavenly smith god and which was found in
   Yamata no Orochi. Does the origin of each object (crafted vs. discovered) suggest
   anything about their different symbolic functions?

## Exercise 2: The Rejected Sister — A Close Reading
**Type:** Reading + Analysis

Find the Rejected Sister episode in your Chamberlain and read it carefully. It is brief.

**Questions to answer:**

1. In Chamberlain's translation, what exact words does Iwanagahime's father use to explain
   what Ninigi has forfeited? Quote the passage (it is short).

2. The father's explanation comes *after* the rejection — Ninigi does not know when he
   makes the choice what he is giving up. How does this timing (consequence revealed after
   action, not before) shape the emotional and moral effect of the episode?

3. The text says Iwanagahime was "ugly." The text does not say she was bad or unworthy.
   What would it mean to choose "rock" (endurance, permanence) over "blossom" (beauty,
   transience)? Write two or three sentences about what the choice symbolizes beyond its
   literal surface.

## Exercise 3: Tracking the Transition from Myth to Legend
**Type:** Analysis

The Kojiki shifts registers across the course of its three books. Even within the Upper
Volume, the narrative feels different in the Izanagi/Izanami section vs. the Ninigi section.

**Write a short paragraph (5–7 sentences) observing the following:**

Compare the narrative style of the opening cosmogony (first generation of deities) with
the Ninigi sections you just read. What has changed? What kinds of events still happen?
What kinds of events stop happening? Do the gods become more or less predictable? What
does this shift suggest about the narrative's awareness that it is moving from mythology
toward history?
