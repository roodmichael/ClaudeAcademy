## Exercise 1: Evidence Basis Classification
**Type:** Analysis / Written

For each situation below, identify the evidence basis (observation, hearsay, inference, gut feeling, logical deduction, or none) and then select the most appropriate Japanese uncertainty expression.

1. **Situation:** You saw dark clouds outside. You want to say it looks like rain.
   - Evidence basis: ___
   - Best expression: ___
   - Japanese sentence: ___

2. **Situation:** Your friend just told you that the café on Main Street closed. You're telling someone else.
   - Evidence basis: ___
   - Best expression: ___
   - Japanese sentence: ___

3. **Situation:** No one's answering Tanaka-san's phone and their jacket isn't on the chair. You infer they've left.
   - Evidence basis: ___
   - Best expression: ___
   - Japanese sentence: 田中さんは___ようです/みたいです (fill in the verb appropriately)

4. **Situation:** Your train was scheduled to arrive at 3:00. It's now 3:10 and it's not here. Something logical must have happened.
   - Evidence basis: ___
   - Best expression: ___
   - Japanese sentence: ___

5. **Situation:** You have absolutely no reason to think it, but you wonder if it might snow tomorrow.
   - Evidence basis: ___
   - Best expression: ___

## Exercise 2: そうだ Disambiguation Drill
**Type:** Analysis / Written

Classify each sentence as Hearsay そうだ or Appearance そうだ. Then identify the formation clue that tells you which it is.

1. 雨が降りそうだ。Formation clue: ___. Type: ___
2. 明日は晴れるそうだ。Formation clue: ___. Type: ___
3. 彼女は嬉しそうだね。Formation clue: ___. Type: ___
4. 田中さんが転職するそうですよ。Formation clue: ___. Type: ___
5. このスープ、おいしそう！Formation clue: ___. Type: ___
6. この店、今日は閉まっているそうです。Formation clue: ___. Type: ___

**What to observe:** In sentences 2 and 4, look at what immediately precedes そうだ. Is it a verb stem or a complete verb form?

## Exercise 5: Production — The Hedging Insertion Exercise
**Type:** Written Production

These sentences make strong unhedged declarative claims. Add appropriate uncertainty expressions based on the evidence situation provided in brackets.

1. 明日は晴れです。[You're just guessing — no weather check] → 明日は___
2. 彼女はもう結婚しています。[You heard this from a mutual friend] → ___
3. このレストランは美味しいです。[You can see from the queue outside it's popular] → ___
4. 会議は3時に始まります。[It says so on the calendar, so logically it should be true] → ___
5. 彼は怒っています。[You can see his expression right now] → ___

After writing each sentence, say it aloud. Notice how the hedged version sounds more natural and socially aware than the flat declarative.
