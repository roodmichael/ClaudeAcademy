## Exercise 1: Contraction Conversion
**Type:** Analysis / Written Production

Below are ten sentences in full formal or plain form. Rewrite each one using the appropriate casual contraction. Write the contracted form next to each.

1. 今、音楽を聞いている。→
2. 忘れてしまった、ごめん。→
3. もう行かなければ。→
4. メモしておく。→
5. どうして泣いているの？→
6. これは私のではない。→
7. 彼はゲームをしているよ。→
8. 早く寝なくてはいけない。→
9. お金を全部使ってしまった。→
10. 今日は勉強するのだ。→

**What to observe:** Did any of them feel unnatural? Which one surprised you? Check your answers with the tutor or the contraction table from the lesson.

## Exercise 2: Register Sorting
**Type:** Analysis / Thought Experiment

Read the following four situations. For each one, decide: would you use contracted casual speech, standard polite speech (ます/です), or is it ambiguous?

**Situation A:** Texting your close friend to say "I already ate."  
Your choice: _____

**Situation B:** Telling your boss at a Japanese company "I have already submitted the report."  
Your choice: _____

**Situation C:** Leaving a comment on a Japanese cooking video online: "I made this and it turned out great!"  
Your choice: _____

**Situation D:** Asking a stranger on the street for directions.  
Your choice: _____

**What to observe:** Situations C and D are genuinely ambiguous in real life — reflect on why. What signals would help you decide which register to use?

## Exercise 3: Oral Shadowing Drill
**Type:** Hands-on / Speaking

Say the following sentences out loud, first in the full form, then in the contracted form. Repeat each pair three times until the contracted form feels natural on your tongue. Speed up each repetition.

1. 見ている → 見てる → 見てる → 見てる
2. 忘れてしまった → 忘れちゃった → 忘れちゃった → 忘れちゃった
3. 行かなければ → 行かなきゃ → 行かなきゃ → 行かなきゃ
4. 覚えておく → 覚えとく → 覚えとく → 覚えとく
5. そうではない → そうじゃない → そうじゃない → そうじゃない
6. どこへ行くのですか → どこへ行くんですか → どこへ行くんですか

**What to observe:** Which form still requires effort to say quickly? Write down the one that feels least automatic — that's your priority for the week.

## Exercise 4: Spot the Contraction
**Type:** Listening / Analysis

Read these casual Japanese sentences and identify every contracted form in each one. Write out the full (uncontracted) form for each.

1. もう行かなきゃ、待ってる人がいるんだよ。
2. あ、財布忘れちゃった！どうしよ。
3. そんなことしてんの？やめといたほうがいいよ。
4. 彼女、今どこにいるんだろ？

**What to observe:** Sentence 3 contains at least two contractions — one of which stacks multiple forms. Can you identify both?
