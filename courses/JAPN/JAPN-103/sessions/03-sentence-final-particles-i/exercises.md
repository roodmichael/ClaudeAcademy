# Session 03 Exercises

Complete these before taking the session quiz.

## Exercise 1: Particle Swap Analysis
**Type:** Analysis / Written

For each sentence below, fill in the blank with both ね and よ, then write one sentence describing the social context where each version would be appropriate.

1. このコーヒー、美味しい＿。
   - With ね: Appropriate when ___
   - With よ: Appropriate when ___

2. 明日、休みだ＿。
   - With ね: Appropriate when ___
   - With よ: Appropriate when ___

3. あの店、もう閉まってる＿。
   - With ね: Appropriate when ___
   - With よ: Appropriate when ___

**What to observe:** Did any pair feel genuinely interchangeable? Which ones and why?

## Exercise 2: Particle Identification
**Type:** Analysis / Reading

Read the following short exchange and identify which particle is used in each underlined slot and why it was chosen. Write your reasoning in one sentence.

> A: この映画、見た？
> B: まだだよ。面白い＿って聞いたけど。 [A: ね / B: ね or よ?]
> A: 面白いよ＿！絶対見て。
> B: そうか、じゃあ見てみようかな＿。

1. Which particle most naturally fills B's first line? Why?
2. A uses よ in their second line — what does this signal about the information?
3. B ends with かな — what does that tell you about B's mental state?

## Exercise 3: Soliloquy vs. Seeking
**Type:** Thought Experiment / Speaking

な and なあ are sometimes directed inward (soliloquy) and sometimes softly directed at the listener (mild agreement-seeking). Say the following sentences out loud and decide: is each one likely a soliloquy, or is the speaker also mildly checking in with someone nearby?

1. いいな、そのカバン。(That bag is nice.)
2. もう少し練習したらよかったな。(I should have practiced a little more.)
3. このスープ、懐かしい味がするな。(This soup has a nostalgic flavor.)
4. 一人でいくのは怖いな。(Going alone is scary.)

**What to observe:** Can you construct a situation where each sentence shifts between soliloquy and soft social address? What changes?

## Exercise 4: Particle Production — Complete the Dialogue
**Type:** Written Production

Fill in each blank with the most appropriate particle from the set taught in this session (ね, よ, よね, な/なあ, かな). Some blanks have more than one defensible answer — note any ambiguous cases.

> 友達A: 最近、日本語どう？上達してる＿？
> 友達B: うーん、まあまあかな＿。でも、会話はまだ難しい＿。
> 友達A: そう＿、でも続けていれば上手になる＿。
> 友達B: そうだといいな＿。あ、この本、面白そう＿！
> 友達A: 知ってる？それ、すごく評判いい＿。

Write your completed dialogue and note any slot where you weren't sure.

## Exercise 5: Oral Production
**Type:** Speaking

Say the following sentences out loud, three times each. Focus on the particle at the end — make it audible and natural, not tacked on.

1. 疲れたね。(We're tired, huh.)
2. あっちだよ、こっちじゃないよ。(It's over there, not here.)
3. 行けるかな。(I wonder if I can go.)
4. いいなあ、羨ましい。(Nice, I'm jealous.)
5. 昨日のパーティー、楽しかったよね。(Yesterday's party was fun, right?)

**What to observe:** Which particle felt least natural to say? That's the one to focus on before Session 04.
