## Exercise 1: Parse the Stack
**Type:** Analysis

Each sentence below contains a stacked contraction. Break it into its component layers and write out the full formal version.

1. 何やってんだよ。
   - Layer 1 contraction: ____
   - Layer 2 contraction: ____
   - Full form: ____

2. もう帰ってんのか？
   - Layer 1 contraction: ____
   - Layer 2 contraction: ____
   - Full form: ____

3. そんなこと知ってたんだ。
   - Layer 1 contraction: ____
   - Layer 2 contraction: ____
   - Full form: ____

**What to observe:** Did you find it easier to parse written contractions than heard ones? The next step is to say each full form, then the contracted version, aloud at natural speed.

## Exercise 2: ら抜き Recognition and Conversion
**Type:** Analysis

For each sentence, identify whether the potential form is standard or ら抜き言葉, then write the standard form.

1. 明日、早く起きれる？ → Standard/ら抜き? _____ Standard form: _____
2. あのドラマ、もう見れた！ → Standard/ら抜き? _____ Standard form: _____
3. このケーキ、食べられますか。 → Standard/ら抜き? _____ Standard form: _____
4. 彼は来れないと思う。 → Standard/ら抜き? _____ Standard form: _____
5. 全部覚えられた！ → Standard/ら抜き? _____ Standard form: _____

**What to observe:** Did you notice that items 3 and 5 are already standard? Identify which ones you would personally use in casual conversation and why.

## Exercise 4: Obligation Compression
**Type:** Hands-on / Oral

Below are five obligations in formal Japanese. Compress each to the most natural casual form (either なきゃ or なくちゃ — your choice) and say them aloud.

1. 宿題をしなければなりません。→
2. 早く起きなくてはいけない。→
3. 電話をかけなければ。→
4. もっと練習しなければならない。→
5. 寝なくてはいけない。→

**What to observe:** Which feels more natural to you — なきゃ or なくちゃ? They're largely interchangeable, but different speakers have mild preferences. Ask the tutor which tends to appear more in the media you like.
