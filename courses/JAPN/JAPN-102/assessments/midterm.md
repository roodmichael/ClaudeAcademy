# Midterm Assessment — JAPN-102
**Covers:** Sessions 01–06

---

## Overview

This midterm tests whether the building blocks from the first half of the course are functioning as an integrated system — not isolated facts you can recall, but tools you can actually use. You will decode kanji compounds, demonstrate vocabulary knowledge in context, analyze register choices, and apply the on'yomi/kun'yomi prediction rules. This is an applied challenge, not a memorization test.

---

## Part A: Vocabulary in Context (Sessions 01–03)

### Task A1 — Formal Compound Verb Recognition

Below is a paragraph from a simulated news article. Identify all formal compound verbs (〜する constructions that replace casual verbs in formal contexts). List each one, its reading, its casual equivalent, and the sentence it appears in.

「市は新しい交通システムを導入することを決定した。関係者は計画の詳細を確認した後、来月から実施する予定だ。交通局は市民に対して、詳細を近日中に通知すると述べた。」

**What to submit:** A table with: compound verb | reading | casual equivalent | sentence

### Task A2 — Abstract Noun Collocations

Write a complete sentence in Japanese using each of the following abstract nouns with its correct collocation verb. Your sentence should be meaningful (not just a grammar pattern) and use vocabulary at or below N3 level.

1. 影響 (with: 与える or 及ぼす)
2. 可能性 (with: がある or を探る)
3. 条件 (with: を満たす or が整う)
4. 結果 (with: が出る or として)

### Task A3 — Register Discrimination

For each pair, identify which word/form belongs in the formal context and which in the casual context. Then write one sentence for each that uses the formal register correctly.

Pairs: 友達/友人 | 使う/利用する | 話す/述べる | 心配する/懸念する

## Part B: Kanji Expansion (Sessions 04–05)

### Task B1 — Radical Identification and Mnemonic Construction

For each of the following 5 kanji, identify the radical, name what the radical represents, and write a mnemonic story of 1–2 sentences connecting the radical meaning to the kanji meaning.

Kanji: 連、現、議、感、対

### Task B2 — Compound Unlocking

Choose ONE of the following kanji: 経、政、決、最

Using jisho.org, find 6 compound words (jukugo) at N3 level or below that use this kanji. For each compound:
- Write the compound in kanji
- Write the reading
- Write the meaning
- Write one example sentence using it

### Task B3 — Inference Under Pressure

Read the following paragraph. You have 2 minutes (set a timer). Answer the two questions without looking anything up.

「最近の調査によると、若者の経済的不安が増加している。政府は対策として支援制度の改善を検討しているが、専門家は根本的な原因の解決が必要だと指摘している。」

Questions:
1. What is increasing among young people?
2. What do experts say is necessary?

After the timer: Note any unknown words. Were they necessary for answering the questions, or could you infer enough from context?

## Part C: On'yomi and Kun'yomi (Session 06)

### Task C1 — Rule Application

Apply the on'yomi/kun'yomi rules to predict the reading type for each of the following 10 items. State which rule you are applying. Then verify on jisho.org and note whether your prediction was correct.

Items: 政策、歌う、人、現代、花火、最初、山田 (name)、経験、新しい、決定

### Task C2 — Dictionary Convention

Look up THREE of the kanji from Task C1 in jisho.org. For each:
- List the on'yomi readings (katakana) and one compound word using each
- List the kun'yomi readings (hiragana) and one standalone word or verb/adjective form using each

### Task C3 — Short Explanation

In 3–5 sentences (English is fine), explain the historical reason why kanji have two reading systems. A strong answer will mention: when kanji arrived, the fact that Japanese was already a spoken language, and the names of the two reading types.

## What to Submit

Submit your responses as a structured write-up to the Tutor. You may use bullet points, tables, and sentences. You do not need to format it like an essay. The Tutor will review each part and provide feedback.

---

## Evaluation Criteria

- **Part A:** Correct identification of formal compound verbs; natural collocation use in sentences; accurate register discrimination
- **Part B:** Meaningful (not nonsensical) mnemonic stories; compound words are correctly read and defined; reading under pressure questions correctly answered
- **Part C:** Correct rule identification; accurate dictionary reading extraction; historically accurate explanation of the two-reading system

---

## Tutor Scoring Guide

**Part A — What strong looks like:** Student correctly identifies すべての formal compound verbs in A1 (導入する, 決定した, 確認した, 実施する, 通知する, 述べた). A2 sentences are natural, not just pattern demonstrations — they describe realistic scenarios using N3 vocabulary. A3 register analysis includes explanation of *why* the formal register is required, not just which word to choose.

**Common weaknesses in Part A:** Missing 述べた as a formal compound verb candidate (it is a kun'yomi verb but functions as a formal register substitute for 言う). Collocation errors in A2 — 可能性を感じる instead of 可能性がある.

**Part B — What strong looks like:** Mnemonics in B1 are memorable and connected to the kanji meaning, even if slightly fanciful. Compound exploration in B2 goes beyond the most obvious compounds. B3 inference paragraph — students should correctly answer "young people's economic anxiety" and "resolving the fundamental cause" even if they don't know 根本的 (fundamental) — context inference from 原因の解決 is sufficient.

**Common weaknesses in Part B:** Generic mnemonics ("the radical means speech and the kanji means speech") that don't create a memorable story. Not using jisho.org's JLPT level filter, resulting in N1/N2 compounds in B2.

**Part C — What strong looks like:** Student applies all four rules correctly to all 10 items in C1, including correctly flagging 山田 as nanori and 花火 as a kun'yomi compound exception. Historical explanation in C3 references the arrival of Buddhism and Chinese scholarship via Korea/China, the native Japanese spoken language already existing, and the wave theory of Chinese importation (Go-on, Kan-on, Tō-on).

**Common weaknesses in Part C:** Applying Rule 1 to 花火 and predicting on'yomi (it is a kun'yomi compound). Missing the dictionary convention question's point — students should be looking for the KATAKANA vs. hiragana distinction in jisho.org entries, not just reading back what the entry says.
