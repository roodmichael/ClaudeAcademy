## Recommended Additional Resources (Not Fetched, Not Cited)

These resources were not fetched during research but are recommended for students who want deeper or more structured study:

**Textbooks:**
- *Shin Kanzen Master N3 読解 (新完全マスター N3 読解)* — The most recommended exam-preparation reading workbook for N3. Structured passages at N3 difficulty with detailed answer-key explanations.
- *Shibatani, M. (1990). The Languages of Japan.* Cambridge University Press — Authoritative academic treatment of Japanese linguistics, including historical layers of kanji adoption.
- *Makino, S. & Tsutsui, M. A Dictionary of Basic Japanese Grammar / A Dictionary of Intermediate Japanese Grammar* — Tier 4 grammar reference; useful for precise grammar point explanations supplementing JAPN-101.

**Study Tools:**
- **Anki** (apps.ankiweb.net) — Required course tool. Free spaced repetition application.
- **NHK News Web Easy** (nhk.or.jp/news/easy) — Required course tool. Free daily authentic reading practice.
- **BCCWJ (Balanced Corpus of Contemporary Written Japanese)** — Academic corpus for Japanese frequency data. Requires institutional access; not used in this course but valuable for advanced N2+ study.
