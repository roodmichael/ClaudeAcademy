# Session 03: High-Frequency N3 Vocabulary II — Business, Abstract Nouns, and Formal Compound Verbs
**Course:** JAPN-102
**Learning Objective:** After this session, you will be able to recognize and use 30+ high-frequency N3 vocabulary words in the domains of business, change and causation, and formal academic or official contexts, including the key distinction between casual and formal compound verbs.

---

## Opening

Here is a sentence from an NHK news article: 政府は新しい政策を実施するため、関係者と協議を重ねた。

If you got through JAPN-101, you can parse the grammar: ため (in order to), 〜ために, the verb compound 重ねた (piled up, i.e., repeatedly held). But can you read the content words? 政府 (government), 政策 (policy), 実施する (to implement), 関係者 (those involved), 協議 (deliberation).

Every one of those content words is an N3 jukugo compound. This session covers the vocabulary category that makes news articles, business documents, and formal explanations readable.

## Business and Economic Vocabulary

**政府 (せいふ)** — government (national government)
政府の方針 (government policy direction)

**政策 (せいさく)** — policy
新しい政策を実施する (implement a new policy)

**法律 (ほうりつ)** — law, legislation
法律に従う (follow the law); 法律を制定する (enact a law)

**権利 (けんり)** — right (legal or civil right)
権利を守る (protect rights); 権利がある (to have the right to)

**利益 (りえき)** — profit, benefit, advantage
利益を上げる (increase profit); 利益を得る (obtain a benefit)

**費用 (ひよう)** — cost, expense
費用がかかる (cost money); 費用を削減する (reduce costs)

**契約 (けいやく)** — contract, agreement
契約を結ぶ (conclude a contract); 契約を解除する (cancel a contract)

**取引 (とりひき)** — transaction, deal, trade
取引先 (business partner/client); 取引をする (conduct business)

**経済 (けいざい)** — economy
経済が成長する (economy grows); 経済的な影響 (economic impact)

**開発 (かいはつ)** — development (of a product, region, software)
新製品を開発する (develop a new product)

**技術 (ぎじゅつ)** — technology, technique, skill
最新技術 (latest technology); 技術が進歩する (technology advances)

**環境 (かんきょう)** — environment
環境を守る (protect the environment); 環境問題 (environmental issues)

## Abstract Nouns — The Core of N3 Reading

These words appear as the subjects and objects of argument in news articles, essays, and JLPT reading passages. They are abstract but they have precise meanings.

**影響 (えいきょう)** — influence, effect, impact
影響を与える (to have an influence on); 影響を受ける (to be influenced by); 影響力 (influence/power)

**可能性 (かのうせい)** — possibility, potential
可能性がある (there is a possibility); 可能性を探る (explore possibilities)

**原因 (げんいん)** — cause (of an event)
原因を調べる (investigate the cause); 事故の原因 (cause of the accident)

**結果 (けっか)** — result, outcome
結果が出る (results come out); その結果 (as a result)

**目的 (もくてき)** — purpose, objective
目的を達成する (achieve an objective); 目的のために (for the purpose of)

**条件 (じょうけん)** — condition, terms, requirement
条件を満たす (meet the conditions); 条件が整う (conditions are in place)

**特徴 (とくちょう)** — characteristic, feature
特徴がある (to have a characteristic); 主な特徴 (main features)

**状況 (じょうきょう)** — situation, circumstances
状況を把握する (grasp the situation); 状況に応じて (according to the situation)

## Formal Compound Verbs: The N3 Register Shift

One of the most visible shifts from N4 to N3 is the appearance of **formal compound verbs** — two-kanji noun + する constructions that replace casual verbs in formal contexts:

| Casual verb | Formal compound verb | Meaning |
|-------------|---------------------|---------|
| 使う | 利用する (りようする) | to use, to utilize |
| やる/する | 実施する (じっしする) | to implement, to carry out |
| 見る | 確認する (かくにんする) | to confirm, to verify |
| 始める | 開始する (かいしする) | to commence |
| 終わる | 終了する (しゅうりょうする) | to conclude, to end (formal) |
| 決める | 決定する (けっていする) | to decide, to determine (official decision) |
| 話し合う | 協議する (きょうぎする) | to deliberate, to discuss (formal) |
| 調べる | 調査する (ちょうさする) | to investigate, to research |
| 知らせる | 通知する (つうちする) | to notify, to inform |
| 手伝う | 支援する (しえんする) | to support, to assist (formal) |

These are not interchangeable — 使う in a news article about government policy would sound casual; 利用する is required. 調べる in an academic report should be 調査する.

**Why this matters for reading:** News articles, official notices, and JLPT reading passages almost always use the formal compound verb. If you only know the casual form, the formal form becomes an unknown word.

## Change and Causation Vocabulary

These words appear constantly in the kind of explanatory prose that dominates N3 reading passages:

**変化 (へんか)** — change, transformation
変化する (to change); 大きな変化 (major change)

**改善 (かいぜん)** — improvement (making something better)
改善する (to improve); 改善策 (improvement measures)

**解決 (かいけつ)** — solution, resolution
問題を解決する (solve a problem); 解決策 (solution)

**対策 (たいさく)** — countermeasure, response
対策を立てる (formulate countermeasures); 対策を取る (take measures)

**促進 (そくしん)** — promotion, facilitation (making something happen faster)
経済発展を促進する (promote economic development)

## Collocations for Abstract Nouns

Abstract N3 nouns do not travel alone. Here are the canonical verb combinations:

| Abstract noun | Natural verb(s) | Unnatural substitute |
|---------------|----------------|---------------------|
| 影響 | 与える、受ける、及ぼす | ~~言う、*持つ~~ |
| 可能性 | がある、を探る、を広げる | ~~*存在する~~ |
| 原因 | を調べる、を究明する | ~~を解決する~~ |
| 結果 | が出る、を出す、として | ~~を感じる~~ |
| 目的 | を達成する、のために | ~~を作る~~ |
| 条件 | を満たす、が整う | ~~を作る~~ |

## Why This Matters

The formal compound verbs and abstract nouns in this session are the building blocks of written Japanese in every formal context — news reporting, business communication, official documents, academic writing, and JLPT reading passages. A student who reaches N3 without them is reading at the same level as someone who reads English news but does not know words like "implement," "conditions," or "outcome." This session closes that gap.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Formal compound verbs (利用する, 実施する, 調査する) replace casual verbs in formal Japanese. They are built on two-kanji compounds and are the dominant verb form in news articles and JLPT passages.
- Abstract nouns like 影響, 可能性, and 条件 require learning with their collocation verbs — 影響を与える, 可能性がある, 条件を満たす.
- Business and economic vocabulary (経済, 政策, 契約, 技術) covers the most common topic domain of JLPT N3 reading passages.
- Change and causation vocabulary (変化, 改善, 解決, 対策) is the vocabulary of argument — the words that explain what happened and why.
