## Exercise 1: Formal Compound Verb Substitution
**Type:** Analysis

Each sentence below uses a casual verb. Rewrite each sentence using the appropriate formal compound verb from the session's table. Provide the reading (hiragana) next to each compound verb.

1. 新しいアプリを**使って**ください。→ 新しいアプリを________ください。
2. 警察が事故の原因を**調べました**。→ 警察が事故の原因を________しました。
3. 会議を**始めます**。→ 会議を________します。
4. 管理者に変更を**知らせて**ください。→ 管理者に変更を________してください。
5. 委員会が予算について**話し合いました**。→ 委員会が予算について________しました。

**What to observe:** These substitutions are not optional in formal contexts — using the casual form in a business email or official document sounds wrong. Notice which formal compound verbs feel familiar and which you haven't seen before.

## Exercise 3: News Headline Comprehension
**Type:** Reading + Analysis

Read these simplified news-style sentences and answer the questions below. All vocabulary comes from Sessions 01–03.

A. 政府は環境問題の解決のために新しい政策を実施した。

B. 経済の変化によって、多くの企業が費用を削減する必要がある。

C. 研究チームが事故の原因を調査している。

Questions:
1. In sentence A, what was the government doing, and why?
2. In sentence B, what is causing companies to reduce costs?
3. In sentence C, who is investigating what?
4. Identify any words in these sentences that you cannot read or do not know. Look them up on Jisho.org and add them to your Anki deck.

**What to observe:** Can you get the gist of these sentences even if some words are unfamiliar? Which words are still unknown? This is a preview of the reading strategy work coming in Sessions 10–11.

## Exercise 4: Thematic Vocabulary Grouping
**Type:** Thought experiment

You have now encountered vocabulary across four thematic domains: Emotions/Relationships (Session 02) and Business/Abstract/Change (Session 03).

Take the following 12 words and group them into two thematic clusters of your own design. Your clusters do not need to match the session groupings — use whatever categories make sense to you.

Words: 不安、経済、信頼、原因、政策、協力、影響、変化、相談、技術、条件、悩み

Write the cluster names and assign each word. Then: for each cluster, write one sentence that uses 2 or more of the words in that cluster together naturally.

**What to observe:** Grouping words by meaning association is a memory strategy. The act of deciding which words "go together" in your mind strengthens retention more than re-reading a list.
