## Exercise 2: Dictionary Convention Practice
**Type:** Hands-on

Go to jisho.org and look up each of the following kanji. For each one, record:
- The on'yomi readings (listed in KATAKANA)
- The kun'yomi readings (listed in hiragana)
- One example compound using the on'yomi
- One example word using the kun'yomi (standalone or with okurigana)

Kanji: 見、話、変、水、出

**What to observe:** For some kanji (like 出), the on'yomi and kun'yomi are quite different from each other. For others, you may notice the on'yomi appears in formal words and the kun'yomi appears in everyday verbs. Is that pattern consistent?

## Exercise 4: Rendaku Recognition
**Type:** Analysis

Each word below contains a rendaku-voiced second element. For each:
1. Identify which consonant was voiced (k→g, s→z, t→d, h→b/p)
2. Identify the original unvoiced reading of the second element
3. Confirm whether this is a kun'yomi compound

Words: 本棚 (ほんだな)、手紙 (てがみ)、花火 (はなび)、人々 (ひとびと)

**What to observe:** Were you able to "hear" the rendaku pattern — to identify the voiced sound as a modified version of the original? This is important for kanji readings in compounds where you expect one sound but hear another.
