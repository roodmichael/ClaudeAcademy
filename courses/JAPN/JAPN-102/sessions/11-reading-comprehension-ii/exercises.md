# Session 11 Exercises

Complete these before taking the session quiz. These exercises are more extended than previous sessions — budget 45–60 minutes.

---

## Exercise 1: Full Skim-Scan-Dive Practice
**Type:** Hands-on (timed)

Go to NHK News Web Easy (nhk.or.jp/news/easy). Choose an article from today or this week.

**Round 1 (Fluency pass — 5 minutes, no dictionary, furigana on):**
Set a timer. Read the full article in under 5 minutes. When the timer goes off, write 3 sentences summarizing what the article was about. Do not go back to check.

**Round 2 (Comprehension questions — 3 minutes):**
Now read the first 3 paragraphs again. For each paragraph, write one comprehension question in English that a test might ask (e.g., "What caused X?" or "What does the author say about Y?"). Then answer your own questions.

**Round 3 (Vocabulary pass — 10 minutes):**
Go back through the article and identify 5 unknown or uncertain words. Look each up on jisho.org. Add 3 of them to Anki as sentence cards using the sentence from the article.

**What to observe:** How much did you understand in Round 1 before the dictionary? Were your Round 2 questions answerable from the article text? Did Round 3 reveal any words you should have known from this course?

## Exercise 2: Timed Trap Answer Analysis
**Type:** Analysis (timed)

Read the following passage in under 2 minutes. Then answer the question and identify the trap answers.

Passage:
「日本では、近年、働き方の変化が大きな話題になっている。特に、テレワーク（在宅勤務）が急速に普及し、多くの会社が従業員に週に2〜3日の在宅勤務を認めるようになった。しかし、すべての仕事がテレワークに適しているわけではない。専門家は、テレワークの効果は職種によって大きく異なると述べており、導入には慎重な検討が必要だと指摘している。」

Question: この文章が最も言いたいことは何ですか。

Options:
A. テレワークはすべての職種で効果的な働き方だ
B. 日本のテレワーク普及は完全に失敗している
C. テレワークの導入は職種によって検討が必要で、すべての仕事に適しているわけではない
D. テレワークは急速に普及しており、今後も増え続けるだろう

Analyze each option:
- Which is a trap? What type of trap?
- Which is the correct answer? Which line in the passage supports it?

## Exercise 3: Referential Expression Tracking
**Type:** Analysis

In the following short passage, every referential expression is underlined. Identify what each refers to.

「山田さんは去年、大きな決断をしました。それ①は仕事を辞めて、新しいビジネスを始めることでした。その②決断の背景には、会社への不満と、自分の可能性を試したいという気持ちがありました。今では、彼女③のビジネスは順調に成長しています。彼女はその④経験を通じて多くのことを学んだと語っています。」

What does each referential expression refer to?
1. それ①
2. その②
3. 彼女③
4. その④

**What to observe:** In each case, the referent is in the immediately preceding sentence or clause. Did you find them quickly? This tracking speed is what makes referential expression questions answerable within the 3-minute reading budget.

## Exercise 4: The Weekly Reading Plan
**Type:** Thought experiment

Design a weekly Japanese reading schedule that fits your actual life. Be realistic about how much time you have.

Constraints:
- Must include 2–3 NHK Web Easy articles per week
- Must include daily Anki review (10–15 minutes minimum)
- Should include 1 weekly kanji jisho.org exploration

Format:
| Day | Activity | Time |
|-----|----------|------|
| Monday | | |
| ... | | |

Then: What is the single most likely obstacle to this schedule? (Be honest: is it time? Is it remembering? Is it a specific session of the week that tends to go off plan?) Write one strategy to address that obstacle.

**What to observe:** Planning is not studying, but it determines whether studying happens. The students who reach N3 are not the ones with the most study time — they are the ones with the most consistent study habits.
