## Exercise 1: Discourse Connective Map
**Type:** Analysis

Go to NHK News Web Easy (nhk.or.jp/news/easy) and find any article from the current week. Read it once with the dictionary/furigana on.

Then: Go back through the article and underline (or list on paper) every discourse connective you find. Classify each one using the categories from the lesson:
- Contrast (しかし, でも, ところが, 一方)
- Causation/result (だから, ので, その結果, なぜなら)
- Addition (また, さらに, それに)
- Summary/conclusion (つまり, 要するに, というわけで)
- Sequence (まず, 次に, 最後に)

Then: Draw a simple flow diagram showing how the article's paragraphs connect. Arrow from one paragraph to the next, labeled with the connective type.

**What to observe:** How many connectives did you find? Does the article have a clear logical structure? Could you reconstruct the article's argument just from the connectives and the first/last sentences of each paragraph?

## Exercise 2: Skim-Scan-Dive Practice
**Type:** Hands-on

Find a medium-length NHK Web Easy article (~200–400 characters). Set a timer for 3 minutes.

Apply skim-scan-dive in this order:
1. READ the article title (10 seconds)
2. READ the first sentence of each paragraph (30 seconds)
3. READ the last sentence (10 seconds)
4. ANSWER: What is this article about? What is the main point?
5. Find one specific fact or detail in the article by going back to a specific paragraph.

After the timer: How much of the article did you understand from just the skim? What did you miss?

**What to observe:** The skim phase should give you the article's topic and direction. The specific fact requires the dive. If you could not do the skim in 30 seconds, practice reading just the first sentences of each paragraph faster.

## Exercise 3: Question-Type Identification
**Type:** Analysis

For each of the following JLPT-style questions, identify the question type (main idea, referential expression, reason/cause, contrast, author's opinion, information retrieval) and describe the strategy you would use.

1. 「この文章で筆者が最も言いたいことは何ですか。」(What is the author most trying to say?)
2. 「第3段落の『それ』は何を指しますか。」(What does 'それ' in paragraph 3 refer to?)
3. 「なぜ〜したのですか。」(Why did ~ happen?)
4. 「このイベントに参加できるのは誰ですか。」(Who can participate in this event? — from a notice)
5. 「筆者はAについてどう思っていますか。」(What does the author think about A?)

**What to observe:** Can you name the strategy before you even see the passage? The question phrasing alone reveals which strategy to apply.

## Exercise 4: Trap Answer Analysis
**Type:** Analysis

Read the following short passage and four multiple choice options. Identify which answer is the trap and explain why. Then identify the correct answer.

Passage:
「最近の研究によると、適度な運動は健康に非常によい影響を与えることが証明されている。しかし、過度な運動は逆に体に負担をかける可能性がある。専門家は、個人の体力に合わせた運動を推奨している。」

Question: この文章が最も言いたいことは何ですか。

Options:
A. 運動は体に必ずよい影響を与える
B. 過度な運動は健康のためによい
C. 個人の体力に合わせた適切な運動が推奨される
D. 運動は最近の研究によって重要性が証明された

**What to observe:** Option A uses 必ず (absolutely) — which extreme word trap does this represent? Option B contradicts the passage directly. Option D is factually true but what is it missing? How do you confirm C against the text?
