# Session 02: High-Frequency N3 Vocabulary I — Emotions, Relationships, and Social Vocabulary
**Course:** JAPN-102
**Learning Objective:** After this session, you will be able to recognize, read, and use 30+ high-frequency N3 vocabulary words in the domains of emotions, mental states, relationships, and social interactions.

---

## Opening

Think about what you would need to say, in Japanese, to describe a difficult conversation you had with someone — a disagreement, a time you asked for help, a moment when you felt uncertain about a decision.

At N4, you probably get about halfway there. You can say basic things happened. But the words for *what it felt like* — the vocabulary of trust, anxiety, disappointment, cooperation, criticism — those are almost all N3 words. This session gives you the emotional and social vocabulary to go from "something happened between us" to "we disagreed, I felt anxious, but in the end we cooperated."

---

## Emotional and Mental State Vocabulary

These words appear frequently in JLPT N3 reading passages and in everyday written Japanese. Learn each with its reading, meaning, and at least one example sentence.

**感情 (かんじょう)** — emotion, feeling (as a category)
感情を表す (express emotions)

**感じる (かんじる)** — to feel, to sense [volitional; takes を]
不安を感じる (feel anxiety)

**気づく (きづく)** — to notice, to become aware of
問題に気づく (notice a problem)

**不安 (ふあん)** — anxiety, unease, worry
将来について不安を感じる (feel anxious about the future)

**心配 (しんぱい)** — worry, concern [more specific than 不安; often about someone/something]
心配する (to worry about); 心配をかける (to cause someone worry)

**悩む (なやむ)** — to worry over, to be troubled by, to agonize [the process of worrying]
進路に悩む (agonize over career path)

**悩み (なやみ)** — a worry, a concern, a problem [noun form of 悩む]
悩みを相談する (consult someone about a worry)

**喜び (よろこび)** — joy, delight [noun]
合格の喜び (the joy of passing)

**喜ぶ (よろこぶ)** — to be delighted, to rejoice

**落ち込む (おちこむ)** — to feel depressed, to feel down
失敗して落ち込む (feel down after failing)

**怒る (おこる)** — to get angry
理由もなく怒る (get angry for no reason)

**驚く (おどろく)** — to be surprised, to be astonished
その知らせに驚いた (was surprised by the news)

## Relationship and Social Vocabulary

**信頼 (しんらい)** — trust, confidence [noun]
信頼を得る (earn trust); 信頼できる人 (a trustworthy person)

**信じる (しんじる)** — to believe, to trust [volitional verb]

**協力 (きょうりょく)** — cooperation, collaboration
協力する (to cooperate); 協力をお願いする (to ask for cooperation)

**協力者 (きょうりょくしゃ)** — collaborator, someone who cooperates

**意見 (いけん)** — opinion, view
意見を述べる (state an opinion); 意見が合う (opinions align)

**批判 (ひはん)** — criticism, critique
批判する (to criticize); 批判を受ける (to receive criticism)

**支持 (しじ)** — support, backing
支持する (to support/endorse); 支持を集める (to gather support)

**関係 (かんけい)** — relationship, connection, relevance
関係がある (to be related/relevant); 関係ない (unrelated, not relevant)

**交流 (こうりゅう)** — exchange, interaction [often cultural or social exchange]
文化交流 (cultural exchange)

**連絡 (れんらく)** — contact, communication
連絡する (to contact); 連絡がある (to hear from someone)

**相談 (そうだん)** — consultation, discussion of a problem
相談する (to consult/talk something over); 相談に乗る (to listen to someone's problem)

## Register in Social Vocabulary: Casual vs. Formal Variants

At N3, the same concept often has two forms — one for casual speech, one for formal contexts. These are not interchangeable:

| Casual | Formal | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| 友達 (ともだち) | 友人 (ゆうじん) | friend |
| 怒る (おこる) | 憤慨する (ふんがいする) | to be angry |
| 話す (はなす) | 述べる (のべる) | to speak/state |
| 心配する | 懸念する (けねんする) | to worry/have concerns |

When writing a formal email or report, 友人 and 懸念 fit; 友達 and 心配する do not. We will study this register dimension more fully in Session 09.

## Collocation Patterns to Learn Now

Words do not travel alone. Here are key collocations for this session's vocabulary — phrases that native speakers habitually use together:

- 信頼を**得る** / 信頼を**失う** — earn trust / lose trust
- 不安を**感じる** — feel anxious (not: 不安を*思う*)
- 意見を**言う** / 意見を**述べる** — say an opinion (casual) / state an opinion (formal)
- 批判を**受ける** — receive criticism
- 相談に**乗る** — to lend an ear, to listen to someone's problem (idiom — literally "get on the consultation")
- 心配を**かける** — to cause worry to someone (e.g., 心配をかけてすみません)

Note: 不安を*思う* is unnatural — 感じる is the correct verb here. This is collocation in action: not all semantically plausible combinations are grammatically natural.

---

## Why This Matters

Emotional and social vocabulary unlocks the reading passages that appear most often on the JLPT N3 and in everyday Japanese: letters, diary entries, opinion essays, informal narratives. More importantly, it unlocks real conversation. You can already construct sentences in Japanese. After this session, the sentences can express something that matters.

---

## Key Takeaways

- Emotional and social vocabulary at N3 includes abstract nouns (感情, 信頼, 不安) that require learning both the word and its collocation partners.
- 悩む (verb) and 悩み (noun) are related but used differently — "to agonize over something" vs. "a worry that troubles you."
- Register distinctions (友達 vs. 友人, 話す vs. 述べる) are vocabulary-level decisions, not just stylistic ones.
- Collocations must be learned with the word: 不安を感じる, 信頼を得る, 相談に乗る.
