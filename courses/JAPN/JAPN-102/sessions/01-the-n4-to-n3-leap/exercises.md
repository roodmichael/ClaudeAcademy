Complete these before taking the session quiz.

## Exercise 1: The Abstraction Audit
**Type:** Analysis

Take 5 minutes and look through a vocabulary list you used during your N4 studies (or just brainstorm 20 N4 words you know well). Write them down.

Now look at the N3 vocabulary categories from the lesson: Business, Government, Science, Nature, Health, Emotions, Change and Causation, and so on.

**Task:** Sort your 20 N4 words into two columns:
- **Concrete/Situational** — things you can see, touch, point to, or act out
- **Abstract/Conceptual** — ideas, relationships, processes, states

**What to observe:** What percentage of your N4 vocabulary is concrete vs. abstract? Now consider: at N3, that ratio flips. Most new vocabulary at N3 is in the abstract column. What does that tell you about how you need to study differently?

Write 2–3 sentences about what you notice.

## Exercise 2: Sentence Card Construction
**Type:** Hands-on

Go to **Jisho.org** and look up these five N3 vocabulary words:

1. 影響 (えいきょう)
2. 可能性 (かのうせい)
3. 改善 (かいぜん)
4. 連絡 (れんらく)
5. 解決 (かいけつ)

For each word, Jisho.org shows example sentences. Find the example sentence you find most useful (it should be one where you already know all the other words, or almost all of them).

**Task:** Write your own simple sentence using each word. Keep all other words at your current level. The goal is I+1: one unknown per sentence.

Example: 影響 → 音楽は気持ちに影響を与えます。(Music has an influence on one's feelings.)

If you have Anki, add these as sentence cards now.

**What to observe:** Were you able to find sentences where you knew most of the other words? If not, note which words are unfamiliar — those belong in your vocabulary list too.

## Exercise 3: Register Discrimination — First Pass
**Type:** Analysis

The lesson described register pairs — the same concept at different formality levels. Here are three pairs. For each one, read the two sentences and answer: which sentence is appropriate in which context?

**Pair 1:** 使う vs. 利用する
- A. 会議室を利用してください。
- B. この辞書をよく使います。

**Pair 2:** 友達 vs. 友人
- A. 山田さんは私の友人です。(Introducing someone at a business event)
- B. 昨日、友達とカフェに行きました。(Telling a friend about your day)

**Pair 3:** 先生 vs. 教師
- A. 鈴木教師は今年の優秀教員賞を受賞しました。(News headline)
- B. 先生、質問があります。(Talking to your teacher)

**Task:** For each pair, write one sentence of your own using the word you think is correct for the formal context, and one sentence using the word for the casual context.

**What to observe:** Can you already feel the register difference intuitively, or does it feel arbitrary? Register will come up constantly in Sessions 02, 03, and 09.

## Exercise 4: 理由 vs. 原因 — First Encounter
**Type:** Analysis

Without looking up the difference (you just read about it in the lesson), try to assign 理由 or 原因 to each of these scenarios. Then write your reasoning.

1. A train was delayed because of a signal malfunction. What does the announcement use: 遅延の___は信号故障です。
2. A student explains to a teacher why they didn't submit homework. 宿題を出さなかった___を説明します。
3. A scientist reports the factor that caused a species to go extinct. 絶滅の___を調査しました。
4. A person tells a friend why they quit their job. 仕事を辞めた___があります。

**What to observe:** Did your intuition match the rule from the lesson (理由 = human decisions; 原因 = events and phenomena)? If you got any wrong, which scenarios tripped you up and why?
