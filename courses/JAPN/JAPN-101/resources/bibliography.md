## Tier 4 — Well-Known Reference Books

These sources were identified and cited in research documents as authoritative textbook resources. They were not fetched directly during research but are fully referenced in the research documents and are recommended as supplementary study materials.

| Source | Author/Org | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| *A Dictionary of Basic Japanese Grammar* | Makino & Tsutsui | Comprehensive formal treatment of N5-N4 grammar; covers te-form, conditionals, and passive with linguistic rigor |
| *A Dictionary of Intermediate Japanese Grammar* | Makino & Tsutsui | N3-N2 grammar; covers causative-passive, potential form, clause connectors in depth |
| *Genki I & II* | Eri Banno et al. | Standard university Japanese textbook; introduces te-form extensions, conditionals, and basic passive |
| *Minna no Nihongo I & II* | 3A Corporation | Popular communicative textbook series covering the same core grammar points |
| *Tobira: Gateway to Advanced Japanese* | Mayumi Oka et al. | Bridges N3 to N2; useful for learners reaching toward advanced reading and clause connection |

## Source Quality Note

No Tier 1–3 sources were located for this material. The JLPT organization (jlpt.jp) does not publish grammar explanations. Japan Foundation materials do not cover individual grammar point explanations at this level. The Wasabi Japanese and Bunpro sources are the best available practitioner references for N4-N3 grammar and are consistent with the Tier 4 textbook content they parallel.

For deeper academic/linguistic treatment, see:
- Shibatani, M. (1990). *The Languages of Japan.* Cambridge University Press. — Covers Japanese typology including relative clauses and aspect
- Makino, S. & Tsutsui, M. (1986). *A Dictionary of Basic Japanese Grammar.* Japan Times. — The definitive English-language reference grammar
