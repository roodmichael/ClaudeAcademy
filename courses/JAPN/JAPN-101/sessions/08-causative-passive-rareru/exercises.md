## Exercise 2: Causative-Passive Sentences
**Type:** Hands-on

Describe being forced to do something using causative-passive. Write Japanese sentences for each scenario. Identify who is doing the forcing (に) and who is being forced (subject).

1. Your boss made you stay late at the office last night. (残業する)
2. As a child, your mother made you eat broccoli. (ブロッコリーを食べる)
3. Your older sibling made you carry all the luggage. (荷物を持つ)
4. I was forced to give a presentation at the meeting. (発表する)

**What to record:** Your four sentences with English translations. Does using causative-passive in each case imply the action was unwanted?

## Exercise 3: The Four Meanings of られる
**Type:** Analysis

Each sentence uses られる (or れる). Identify which of the four meanings applies — passive, potential, spontaneous, or respectful — and explain your reasoning.

1. 先生が来られました。
2. この問題は難しくて解かれません。
3. 昔のことが思い出される。
4. 私のお菓子が誰かに食べられた！
5. 部長が会議室に入られました。
6. 私はスシが食べられます。
7. 彼女の優しさに感動させられた。(I was moved by her kindness.) — *Which type is this?*

**What to record:** Your classification for each (Passive/Potential/Spontaneous/Respectful), a one-sentence explanation, and the signals you used to disambiguate.

## Exercise 4: Disambiguation Practice
**Type:** Analysis

This exercise uses ambiguous sentences. Without additional context, identify the most likely meaning of られる and explain which contextual signals you are using.

1. 食べられますか。
2. 見られた。
3. 先生が話された。
4. 彼が尊敬される理由が分かる。(I understand why he is respected.)
5. 故郷が思い出される。

For each, also describe what additional context would resolve the ambiguity if you aren't sure.

## Exercise 5: Forced Experiences
**Type:** Thought experiment (short writing)

Think of three real or hypothetical situations where you were forced to do something you didn't want to do — by a boss, parent, teacher, or circumstance. Describe each using causative-passive.

Write each sentence in:
- Past tense (what happened)
- Plus one additional sentence explaining your reaction or how you felt using grammar from earlier in the course (てしまう? のに? たら?)

**What to record:** Your three causative-passive sentences plus three follow-up sentences.
