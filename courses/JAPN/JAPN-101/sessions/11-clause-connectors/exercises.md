# Session 11 Exercises

Complete these before taking the session quiz.

---

## Exercise 1: から or ので?
**Type:** Analysis

For each situation, decide whether から or ので is more appropriate and explain why.

1. Texting a close friend to explain why you will be late: 「遅くなる\_\_\_、先に食べていて。」
2. Emailing your manager to explain you need to leave early: 「体調が悪い\_\_\_、早退させていただきたいのですが。」
3. Answering a child's "why" question out loud: 「なぜ空は青いの？ → 光が散乱する\_\_\_だよ。」
4. Responding to なぜですか from a teacher: 「\_\_\_です。」(complete this — which form anchors the answer?)
5. A weather report explaining why to bring an umbrella: 「午後に雨が降るでしょう\_\_\_、傘を持って行ってください。」

## Exercise 2: Contrast Connectors — Which One?
**Type:** Analysis

Choose between のに, けど, or が for each sentence. Explain your reasoning, especially when のに would or would not work.

1. このカフェは遠い\_\_\_、毎週来る。(neutral: I go often despite the distance)
2. 一生懸命練習した\_\_\_、試合に負けた。(frustrated: I lost despite practicing hard)
3. すみません\_\_\_、道を教えていただけますか。(polite: excuse me, but...)
4. 高い\_\_\_、品質がいい。(neutral: expensive but good quality)
5. 疲れている\_\_\_、眠れない。(frustrated: tired but can't sleep)
6. 高い\_\_\_買わないで。(don't buy it, even though it's expensive — emotional)

## Exercise 3: たり〜たりする
**Type:** Hands-on

1. Describe your ideal day off using たり〜たりする with at least four activities.

2. Describe the weather this week using the alternating-states usage: 「\_\_\_たり\_\_\_たりしている」

3. Now rewrite your day-off description using te-form chaining (て-form). Compare the nuance:
   - Which version implies you did everything in that order?
   - Which implies you did those things among others, in no particular sequence?

**What to record:** Your たり sentence, your alternating weather sentence, and your te-form comparison with nuance analysis.

## Exercise 4: ながら Sentence Construction
**Type:** Hands-on

Combine each pair of actions into a ながら sentence. First, verify that the same subject performs both actions and that both are genuinely simultaneous.

1. 私: コーヒーを飲む / メールをチェックする
2. 彼: 歩く / 電話で話す
3. 母: 料理する / 先生: 電話する → *Can this use ながら? Why not?*
4. 私: 音楽を聴く / 走る
5. 彼女: 宿題をする / テレビを見る

For each valid ながら sentence, note which action is the background (before ながら) and which is the main activity (after ながら). Does the choice of ordering change the nuance?

## Exercise 5: Integration — Use Five Connectors
**Type:** Hands-on (writing)

Write a short paragraph (5-7 sentences) describing a real or invented situation where things did not go as planned — a trip, a project, a cooking attempt, a training session.

Requirements:
- Use から or ので at least once (choose based on register)
- Use が or けど once (neutral contrast)
- Use のに once (emotional contrast — something that frustrated or surprised you)
- Use たり〜たりする once (to describe multiple things that happened)
- Use ながら once (to describe doing two things at once)

**What to record:** Your paragraph with each connector underlined or labeled, plus English translation.
