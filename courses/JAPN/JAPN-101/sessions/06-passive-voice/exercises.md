## Exercise 2: Active to Passive Transformation
**Type:** Hands-on

Convert these active sentences to passive. Identify what becomes the subject, what the agent particle is, and why you chose it.

1. 田中さんがその論文を書いた。(Tanaka-san wrote that paper.)
   → Passive using によって (formal attribution)

2. 先生が生徒を褒めた。(The teacher praised the student.)
   → Passive using に

3. 犬が子供を噛んだ。(The dog bit the child.)
   → Passive (child's perspective)

4. 友達がプレゼントをくれた。(My friend gave me a present.)
   → Passive using から

**What to record:** The passive version of each sentence, English translation, and the agent particle used with a brief explanation.

## Exercise 3: Direct vs. Adversative Passive
**Type:** Analysis

These sentences all use passive voice, but some are direct passive and some are adversative/indirect passive. Classify each and explain your reasoning:

1. 私の自転車が盗まれた。(My bicycle was stolen.)
2. 子供に泣かれて眠れなかった。(The child cried and I couldn't sleep.)
3. この映画はキャメロンによって監督された。(This film was directed by Cameron.)
4. 電車の中で隣の人に話しかけられた。(The person next to me spoke to me on the train.)
5. 雨に降られて服が濡れた。(It rained on me and my clothes got wet.)
6. 上司に残業を頼まれた。(I was asked to work overtime by my boss.)

**What to record:** Direct (D) or Adversative (A) for each, and your reasoning — especially for the adversarial cases, identify what makes it adversarial (intransitive verb? indirect effect?).

## Exercise 4: Choosing the Agent Particle
**Type:** Analysis

Fill in the correct agent particle: に, から, or によって.

1. この絵は毕加索（ピカソ）\_\_\_描かれた。(This painting was painted by Picasso.)
2. 私は先生\_\_\_宿題をチェックされた。(I had my homework checked by the teacher.)
3. このルールは委員会\_\_\_決められた。(This rule was decided by the committee.)
4. 荷物は友達\_\_\_受け取られた。(The package was received from a friend.) — *Is passive natural here?*
5. 新しい薬がその研究者\_\_\_発見された。(The new medicine was discovered by that researcher.)

**What to record:** Your particle choice for each and your reason. For #4, explain whether passive is the most natural construction.

## Exercise 5: Adversative Passive — Expressing Being Affected
**Type:** Hands-on (short writing)

Adversative passive allows you to describe how you were negatively affected by someone else's actions. Write four sentences using adversative passive describing situations where:

1. A neighbor played loud music until late at night
2. A friend cancelled plans at the last minute
3. It rained on you while you were walking
4. Someone next to you on a train talked loudly on the phone the whole time

Then add one extra challenge: write a sentence where the emotion is *positive* rather than negative — can adversative passive work for positive surprise? What changes, if anything?
