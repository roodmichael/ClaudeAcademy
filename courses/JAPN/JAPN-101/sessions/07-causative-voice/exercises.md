## Exercise 2: に vs. を — Letting or Making?
**Type:** Analysis

Each blank should be filled with に or を depending on whether the situation describes permission or compulsion. Explain your choice.

1. 先生は生徒\_\_\_もう帰らせた。(The teacher ____ the students go home already.)
   - Context A: The students asked and the teacher agreed → permission
   - Context B: The teacher decided abruptly to end class early → neutral/ambiguous

2. 親は子供\_\_\_公園で遊ばせた。(The parent ____ the child play in the park.)
   - What nuance does your particle choice create?

3. 上司は社員\_\_\_残業させた。(The boss ____ the employees work overtime.)
   - Which particle is more likely if the employees resented it?
   - Which particle if the context is ambiguous?

**What to record:** Your particle choice for each, the nuance it creates, and notes on any context-dependence.

## Exercise 3: Transitive Verb Causative
**Type:** Hands-on

With transitive verbs, the person must take に (cannot use を for both person and object). Write causative sentences for these scenarios:

1. The mother made the child eat spinach. (子供, ほうれん草, 食べる)
2. The teacher had the students write the answer on the board. (生徒, 答え, 書く)
3. The company made the employee read the manual. (社員, マニュアル, 読む)
4. Mom let the children watch TV. (子供, テレビ, 見る)

**What to record:** Your four sentences. For #4, note whether your sentence communicates "let" or "make" — and how you know.

## Exercise 4: Causative Requests
**Type:** Hands-on

Write these polite requests using causative + てください or てもらえますか:

1. Please let me try this dish. (taste = 食べてみる)
2. Please let me explain. (説明する)
3. Please let me see that document. (書類, 見る)
4. Could you let me take a photo here? (写真, 撮る)
5. Please let me know when you decide. (決まる, 知らせる)

Write each in both: casual (てください) and formal (ていただけますか) versions.

## Exercise 5: Causative in Social Context
**Type:** Thought experiment

Consider these three situations involving causation:

**Situation 1:** You are a manager. Your team worked late to finish a critical project deadline because you required it.

**Situation 2:** You are a parent. You allowed your child to skip school one day to go to a special event.

**Situation 3:** You are a teacher. You assigned students an exercise they had to do in front of the class.

For each situation:
a) Write a Japanese sentence describing what happened using causative voice
b) Identify which particle (に or を) is on the person being caused, and why
c) Is this "letting" or "making"? Does the social relationship affect how you frame it?

**What to record:** Your three sentences with explanations.
