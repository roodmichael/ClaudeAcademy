# Session 10 Exercises

Complete these before taking the session quiz.

---

## Exercise 1: Nominalization Formation
**Type:** Hands-on

Nominalize each phrase using both こと and の. Mark any na-adjectives or nouns that require な before the nominalizer.

1. 毎朝ヨガをする (doing yoga every morning)
2. 日本語が上手な (being good at Japanese)
3. 彼女が笑う (her smiling)
4. 学生である (being a student)
5. 早く起きる (waking up early)

Then use two of your nominalized phrases in complete sentences.

---

## Exercise 2: こと or の — Free Choice or Forced Choice?
**Type:** Analysis

For each sentence, decide whether こと, の, or either is correct. If only one works, explain why.

1. ピアノを弾く\_\_\_が好きです。
2. 趣味は料理をする\_\_\_だ。
3. 彼が来る\_\_\_を知っていた。
4. 子供たちが走っている\_\_\_を見た。
5. 運動する\_\_\_ができます。(ability)
6. タバコを吸う\_\_\_をやめた。
7. 早く起きる\_\_\_が大切だ。
8. 彼女が歌う\_\_\_を聞いた。(You could hear her from the other room right now)

## Exercise 3: Fixed こと Expressions
**Type:** Hands-on

Write sentences using each of these fixed expressions. Make sure each sentence is natural and complete.

1. ことができる (can do — use a skill you have)
2. ことがある (have experienced — past experience)
3. ことにする (decide to — a recent personal decision)
4. ことになる (externally decided — something that has been arranged)
5. ことにしている (make it a rule to — a habit you maintain)

**What to record:** Five sentences with English translations. Note: in which of these expressions could you NOT substitute の?

---

## Exercise 4: Concrete vs. Abstract
**Type:** Thought experiment

Apply the heuristic: の = concrete/sensory; こと = abstract/conceptual.

For each scenario, choose こと or の and explain your reasoning using the concrete/abstract distinction:

1. You are walking past a window and notice someone playing guitar inside — you mention it.
2. You receive an email and find out that a friend has been promoted — you tell someone about this.
3. You stop a bad habit (something you used to do regularly).
4. You decide to start studying Spanish next year.
5. You watch your coffee brew slowly — you describe observing this process.

---

## Exercise 5: Error Correction
**Type:** Analysis

Find and correct the nominalization error in each sentence:

1. 趣味は旅行するのだ。(My hobby is traveling.)
2. 子供が泣くことを聞こえた。(I could hear the child crying — direct perception)
3. 毎朝ジョギングすることをやめた。(I stopped jogging every morning.)
4. 彼が来ることができた。(He was able to come.) — *Is this actually wrong?*
5. 友達が料理しますのを手伝った。(I helped my friend cook.)
