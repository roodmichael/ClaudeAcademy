## Exercise 1: と — Natural Consequence Test
**Type:** Analysis

For each pair of events, decide whether と is appropriate. If yes, write the sentence. If no, explain why と does not fit.

1. Press a button → a bell rings (every time, automatic)
2. Go to the supermarket → buy milk (you want to, but might forget)
3. Temperature drops below 0°C → water freezes
4. Get hungry → eat bread (your habit)
5. If it's sunny → want to go to the park (desire)
6. Turn the corner → there was a temple (past narrative discovery)

**What to record:** For each: appropriate or not, and either the Japanese sentence or the reason と cannot be used.

## Exercise 3: Spot the Error
**Type:** Analysis

Each sentence contains a misuse of と or ば. Identify the error and correct it (hint: some need たら, which you will learn in Session 05, but you can recognize the problem even before learning the replacement).

1. 時間があると観光したいです。(If I have time, I want to sightsee.)
2. お酒を飲めば運転しないでください。(If you drink, please don't drive.)
3. もし雨が降ると傘を持って行って。(If it rains, take an umbrella.)
4. 宿題が終われば、映画を見ましょう。(When homework is done, let's watch a movie.)

**What to record:** For each: what the error is, why it is an error, and what the sentence *should* use (even if you write "use a different conditional — not ば").

## Exercise 4: と for Discovery (Narrative)
**Type:** Hands-on (short writing)

The "and then" use of と is powerful in narrative. Write three sentences using this pattern to describe what you discovered when you did something:

- Structure: [action in plain present form] と [past result]
- Example: 冷蔵庫を開けると、何もなかった。(I opened the refrigerator and there was nothing there.)

Write three sentences about:
1. Opening something and discovering what was inside
2. Arriving somewhere and finding who was there
3. Trying something and discovering it was a certain way

**What to record:** Your three sentences with English translations. Note: what verb tense appears in the result clause in all three?

## Exercise 5: と vs. ば — Semantic Choice
**Type:** Thought experiment

These sentence pairs cover similar ground with と or ば. For each pair, explain which sounds more natural and why:

1. a) 春になると花が咲く。b) 春になれば花が咲く。
2. a) 薬を飲むと治ると思う。b) 薬を飲めば治ると思う。
3. a) お金があると旅行する。b) お金があれば旅行する。

Hint: Consider whether the result is automatic/universal (favors と) or whether the condition is being presented as the key variable (favors ば).

**What to record:** Your preference for each pair and your reasoning.
